THE LORE

When the Veil tore, the world learned what it was made of.

The HALCYON worlds were built on crystal — the living lattice that lights their cities, powers their fleets and remembers their dead. For an age the crystal slept in the deep and the nations above it only argued about who it belonged to.

Then the Veil tore. A rift opened at the heart of the old altar plain and the Mawborn came through it: a pit-horde of black chitin and ember, ripper packs and brood-wyrms, led by things that used to be people. Where they walk, the crystal goes dark.

The Dawnmarch answered first — knights and storm-priests who believe the crystal is holy and the rift is a wound to be closed. VECTRA answered second, and louder: a machine fleet that sees the crystal as fuel and the war as an engineering problem — nanite walkers, sky-dreadnoughts, an orbital ring that can erase a valley.

And now something has stepped through the Veil that belongs to neither side. A Titan who wears the rings of a dead order and fights for reasons of his own. The altar is the only place where all of them meet — whoever holds it holds the crystal's voice. Three against three. Hold the circle, or burn.

DAWNMARCH

Order of light. Paladins and storm-elders; shields, sunbursts, a beam that ends arguments. They hold the line and they keep their word.

VECTRA

Machine fleet. Goliath walkers, the Battlecruiser, the Halo station. Precision, heat, and the patience to target from orbit.

MAWBORN

The pit-horde. Rippers, brood-wyrms, brutes, and the Swarm Queen who calls brimstone down from the dark. Every enemy you face wears their skin.

THE TITAN

Korvax. Neither side. Violet lightning, rune-rings, ring portals — and a singularity that swallows the fight whole.

THE LEGENDS

Six kits. Every ability reads before it lands.

Every legend starts at level 6 with the whole kit live. Five-second cooldowns. Three-second respawns. Your own legend is harder to kill.
